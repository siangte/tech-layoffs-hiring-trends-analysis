# 📊 Tech Industry Layoffs and Hiring Trends Analysis (2024–2026)

## Overview
This project analyzes workforce trends in the technology industry from **2024 to 2026** using Tableau. The dashboard explores layoffs, hiring activity, AI adoption, and AI-related job risks to understand how artificial intelligence and market conditions are reshaping employment in the tech sector.

The project aims to answer common questions from job seekers, including:
- Will AI replace tech jobs?
- Which companies are hiring despite layoffs?
- What are the main reasons behind workforce reductions?
- Which roles face the highest AI automation risk?

---

## Objectives
- Analyze technology industry layoff and hiring trends.
- Compare workforce changes across companies and industries.
- Examine AI adoption and AI-related job risk.
- Identify the primary reasons for layoffs.
- Build an interactive dashboard to communicate key workforce insights.

---

## Dashboard Features
- 📈 Layoff trends over time
- 💼 Hiring trends by company and industry
- 🤖 AI adoption and AI job risk analysis
- 🏢 Company workforce comparison
- 📊 Industry-level analysis
- 📌 Key findings and recommendations for job seekers

## Dataset
**Source:** Kaggle – Tech Layoffs & Hiring Trends

The dataset includes:
- Technology companies
- Industry classifications
- Layoff data
- Hiring activity
- AI adoption metrics
- AI job risk scores
- Workforce changes (2024–2026)

---

## Key Insights
- Despite significant layoffs, many technology companies continued hiring for strategic technical roles.
- AI is changing skill demand rather than eliminating all technology jobs.
- Workforce reductions were primarily driven by restructuring, cost optimization, and changing business priorities.
- AI, cloud computing, cybersecurity, and data-related positions remain among the most in-demand roles.
